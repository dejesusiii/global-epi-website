Who We Are

A public health corporation built to close the distance between evidence and access.

Founded in 2023 by healthcare professionals, GLOBAL EPI LLC delivers evidence-based public health services to communities and populations — with data as the primary axis of every engagement.

M

Our Mission

To transform the health of communities and populations throughout the life course, where data serves as the primary axis to guarantee an optimal state of health.

V

Our Vision

To position ourselves as the leading public health corporation providing consulting, coordination, project management, and health advocacy services, in alignment with quality standards, based on the 10 Essential Public Health Services, and addressing the social determinants of health to ensure continuous community transformation.

Our Approach

Collect. Analyze. Translate.

Data only changes outcomes when it survives the journey from the field to the decision. Our methodology is built around that journey — and we do not hand off a finding until a partner can act on it.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Surveillance data, community assessment, and primary field collection — designed so that the populations most often left out of the record are counted first.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Demographic, epidemiological, and economic analysis that identifies where the burden sits, which determinants drive it, and what a measurable improvement would look like.

  3. 03

    Translate

    Findings converted into programs, policy, training, and communication — built so partner organizations can sustain the work after the engagement ends.

Core Values

Five commitments that govern how we practice.

  • Global Health

    Demographic, epidemiological, and economic analysis applied across borders and populations.

  • Leadership

    Empowerment, collaboration, and governance that build capacity rather than dependency.

  • Communication

    Cultural competence and assertive dissemination, in the language the community actually uses.

  • Interprofessional Practice

    Reducing structural biases, social inequalities, and racism through shared accountability.

  • Systems Thinking

    Solving public health problems for hard-to-reach populations at the level of the system.

Social Determinants of Health

Health outcomes are decided long before the clinic.

We build every engagement around the five determinant domains — because addressing them is the difference between treating a population and transforming it.

Domain 1 of 5

Economic Stability

Employment, income, expenses, debt, and food security shape health long before a clinical encounter takes place. We quantify economic exposure at the neighborhood level and build it directly into program design, rather than controlling it away.
